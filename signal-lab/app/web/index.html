How to follow a signal

  1. Entry: the open of the next bar after the card appears — or market at once if the card is younger than one bar. If price already ran past the target, skip it.
  2. Size: risk the printed 1 % only. Quantity = risk ÷ (entry − stop).
  3. Stop and target: exactly the two numbers on the card. No widening, no averaging down.
  4. Invalidation: if the bar after entry closes beyond the stop, get out even if the wick did not touch it.
  5. Concurrency: at most three trades at once, so one bad hour cannot take the account.
  6. When to stop: if the live window's accuracy CI falls below the break-even line shown on the card, stop trading this model and re-measure.

Reward : risk — where the money is

Accuracy on its own decides nothing. What decides is accuracy against the accuracy you need, and the accuracy you need falls as the reward grows:

break-even accuracy = (1 + fee in R) / (1 + reward : risk)

A 1 : 2 trade with a ~0.06 R fee needs 35.2 %. A 1 : 4 needs 21.1 %. A 1 : 5 needs 17.7 %. The same model that was hopeless at 1 : 2 is profitable at 1 : 4, because it only has to be right once in five instead of once in three — and the fee stops eating the prize: at a 1 ATR stop it is 1.4 % of the reward, against 28 % at the 0.5 ATR stop the old build used.

Even a flawless 100 % rule loses money here. The prize is R, the round trip costs R — the fee is % of the prize. A perfect record therefore returns R per trade. That is not a bug in the search; it is what "0.05 ATR target against a 4 ATR stop" means once an exchange takes its cut.

What is measured, and where

  • Accuracy per band: 2022, 2023 and 2024–2026, non-overlapping trades, Wilson interval.
  • The 100 % rules: mined on ≤ 2023 only, then scored on 2024+ and on ETH.
  • The frontier: no model at all — the arithmetic of the barrier geometry.
  • Fees: 5 bp round trip, charged on every trade, win or lose.

What is not claimed

  • No 98–100 % win rate. The search produced thousands; they are on the previous tab, with their out-of-sample record and their fee arithmetic.
  • Long side only in this build. The short side is the same pipeline on mirrored bars — not shippped until it passes the same three folds.
  • Two symbols in the study. The live engine can watch any MEXC contract, but a symbol the model has not seen is an assumption, not a measurement.

Reproduce every number on this page

cd signal-lab
../.venv/bin/python tools/build_dataset.py          # 9.6 M one-minute bars, BTC + ETH
../.venv/bin/python research/01_frontier.py         # the accuracy / fee frontier
../.venv/bin/python research/04_hundred.py --tf 1h --target 0.05 --stop 4   # the 100 % club
../.venv/bin/python research/05_walkforward.py      # three folds, worst-fold test
../.venv/bin/python research/06_bands.py            # bands + the shipped model
../.venv/bin/python app/server.py --replay --speed 25 --port 8811
