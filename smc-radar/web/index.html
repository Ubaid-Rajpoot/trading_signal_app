Signals –

Everything the model calls a signal right now: waiting for the entry, or already filled.

what the badges mean
LIVE - the plan is open (waiting for the entry, or filled and running).
TARGET HIT - price reached the target printed on the card. The R booked is the plan's own R:R, before fees.
STOPPED - price reached the stop printed on the card. -1R.
MITIGATED - price came back into the block. If the plan had filled, its target/stop decided the R.
INVALIDATED - a candle closed beyond the block, so the level that made this setup strong is gone. For a filled plan that means the stop was taken (-1R); for one that never filled, it means the trade never triggered at all.
INTERRUPTED / NOT WATCHED - the scanner restarted, or the instrument was removed, while the plan was still open. The row is kept, it is simply no longer being followed.
