Trend & structure — where the setup sits

The setup only counts inside a trend: higher highs and higher lows for a long, lower highs and lower lows for a short. A setup buried inside internal structure that is itself inside internal structure is skipped. Measured effect and limits: docs/trend-rules.md.

depth 1 = an external break · 2 = the first internal break inside the trend (allowed) · 3+ = internal within internal (skipped).

Confirm the break: HVB, volume, or both

The TDIGM HVB is the price line breaking the centre band. A high-volume bar is the candle doing it on real participation. In all mode every switched-on condition must hold; in score mode they are added up and the total has to reach the threshold.
